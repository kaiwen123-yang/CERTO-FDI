\title{Recoverable common-noise input models: deficit order $H^2$}
\author{Independent M2S supplement v1}
\date{2026-10-07}
\maketitle
This standalone supplement preserves the frozen M2S v1 model and theorem.
Its full proof and exact source bindings are in
\texttt{m2s\_recoverable\_order\_v1.md}.
Compilation must be verified independently of the mathematical checks.

Fix a strictly stable matrix $A_K$, a full-column noise factor
$G\in\mathbb R^{d\times p}$, and $0\ne B\in\operatorname{range}G$.
Write $f=G^\dagger B$, $F=\|f\|^2>0$.
The known initialized plant is
$x_j=A_Kx_{j-1}+B(a_j^h+g_jb_j^h)+G\xi_j$,
with independent standard Gaussian $\xi_j$.
The fixed prefix is fully read, $a=0,g=1$; afterwards
$a_j=\rho_0+\eta j$, $\rho_0\ge0$, $\eta>0$.
Held tasks have $g=\pm1$, missing profiles satisfy $|g|\le1$.
Both complete healthy paths have rate at most $r/2$, $r>0$,
and unbounded initial level. The upper bound assumes known bidirectional
exact-$k$ moves, reusable and concatenable with holds.
Calendars and profiles are chosen offline.

For each actual input interval of length $m$, set
\[
H_m=[A_K^{m-1}G,\ldots,G],\quad W_m=H_mH_m^\top,\quad
P_m=H_m^\top W_m^\dagger H_m .
\]
The frozen common-support proof identifies the actual retained-state
experiment with the disjoint latent-input projection $P$.
In particular, it retains cross-block state covariance and does not reset
the plant at artificial boundaries. Put
\[
\theta=(fa_j)_j,\quad H_gz=(fg_jz_j)_j,\quad
G_H(\pi)=\frac12\inf_{|\Delta z|\le r}\|P(\theta-H_gz)\|^2,
\quad O_H=\frac12\|\theta\|^2,\quad
D_H^*=O_H-\sup_\pi G_H(\pi).
\]
The information is KL, hence the factor $1/2$.

\begin{theorem}
Under this fixed contract, if
$\beta_k=(k+1)F-M_{k+1}^\top W_{k+1}^\dagger M_{k+1}=0$,
where $M_m=\sum_{q=0}^{m-1}A_K^qB$, then there are fixed
$0<c<C<\infty$ and $H_0$ such that
$cH^2\le D_H^*\le CH^2$ for every integer $H\ge H_0$.
No sharp $H^2$ constant is asserted.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The frozen projection lemmas give $\beta_0=0$, monotonicity,
$\beta_\ell/\ell\to F>0$, and
\[
L_z=\max\{m:\beta_{m-1}=0\}<\infty,\quad
b_+=\inf_{m>L_z}\frac{\beta_{m-1}}m>0,\quad
P_m(\mathbf1_m\otimes f)=\mathbf1_m\otimes f
\ \Longleftrightarrow\ m\le L_z .
\]
Here $L_z\ge k+1\ge2$. For an affine span with center amplitude $A\ge0$,
\[
\|(I-P_m)\theta_m\|^2
\ge \beta_{m-1}A^2-C_K\eta Am ,
\]
where $C_K<\infty$ is a fixed plant constant. This bound does not
require reflection symmetry.

For the upper bound choose fixed $L>n_0+k+2$ and maintain
$M_t=\sum_{j=-n_0+1}^t g_j$.
Hold the current sign $s$ until $sM_t\ge L$.
When at least $k+2$ slots remain, execute an exact-$k$ move and flip
the sign; otherwise hold to $H$.
Threshold overshoot is less than one; a move changes $M$ by at most $k$.
All complete opposite hold runs have lengths between
$2L-k$ and $2L+k+2$, the initial run is at least $L-n_0>2$,
and a skipped final move adds at most $k+1$ holds.
Thus $|M_t|\le L+k+2$ and every post hold run has at least two slots
for large $H$. If $N_h,N_m$ are held-input and move counts, then
$H=N_h+kN_m$, $N_h\ge2N_m$.
The true singleton count is $S=N_h-N_m\ge H/(k+2)$:
the right retained point of a gap is not a singleton.

Let $m=k+1$. Since its constant mode is preserved, only the affine
slope is lost in an exact gap:
\[
E=\|(I-P)\theta\|^2
\le N_m\eta^2F\,m(m^2-1)/12=O(H).
\]
Put $h=(g_jf)_j$, $w=P\theta$, $p=Ph$, and
$\lambda_j=h_j^\top w_j$.
On singletons $\lambda_j=Fg_ja_j$; on the fixed-length gaps the
error in this equality is uniformly bounded independently of $a_j$.
The post cumulative $S_t=\sum_{j=1}^t g_j$ is bounded, so Abel's identity
\[
\sum_{j=1}^t g_ja_j=a_tS_t-\eta\sum_{j=1}^{t-1}S_j
\]
shows that all partial $\lambda$ sums are $O(t+1)$ and
$N=\sum_j\lambda_j=O(H)$.
Singletons give $D=\|p\|^2\ge FS\ge FH/(k+2)$, while
$D\le F(H+n_0)$.
Define $\alpha=N/D=O(1)$ and $v=w-\alpha p$.
Then $Pv=v$, $\sum_jh_j^\top v_j=0$, and orthogonality gives
\[
\|\theta-v\|^2=E+\alpha^2D=O(H).
\]
The per-input scalar correction $h_j^\top p_j$ is uniformly bounded
because every gap has fixed length. Thus repaired partial masses
remain $O(t+1)$.
The complete Lipschitz class with free level has support
\[
h_{\mathcal Z}(\lambda')
=r\sum_{t=-n_0+1}^{H-1}
 \left|\sum_{j=-n_0+1}^t\lambda'_j\right|=O(H^2),
\qquad \lambda'_j=h_j^\top v_j .
\]
The zero total mass is essential. The range condition makes $v$
an observable statistic of the actual retained states.
The dual inequality
\[
O_H-G_H(\pi)\le
\frac12\|\theta-v\|^2+h_{\mathcal Z}(\lambda')
\]
proves the upper bound.

For the lower bound terminal unread moves are dominated by holding,
so it suffices to treat endpoint-$H$ calendars, whose true intervals
cover all post inputs. Set
$\varepsilon=(4L_z+2)^{-1}$, $J_0=\lceil\varepsilon H\rceil$.
If some positive-penalty span ends at $t\ge J_0$, its center satisfies
$A\ge\eta\varepsilon H/2$ even for long spans.
For large $H$, the affine inequality and $\beta_{m-1}\ge b_+m$ imply
\[
O_H-G_H(\pi)\ge\frac12\|(I-P)\theta\|^2
\ge \frac{b_+\eta^2\varepsilon^2}{16}H^2 .
\]
Here $z=0$ bounds the profiled information by the observed oracle.

Otherwise every span ending at $t\ge J_0$ has length at most $L_z$
and preserves its constant mode. There are at least
$(1-\varepsilon)H/L_z-O(1)$ such late spans, with center amplitude
at least $\eta\varepsilon H/2$ for large $H$.
Choose the complete legal path $z_j=(r/2)g_j$.
It obeys $|\Delta z|\le r$ and is realizable by the two rate-$r/2$
healthy paths. Write
$\widetilde h_j=(r/2)fg_j^2$.
For a late zero-penalty span,
\[
\ip{P_m\theta_m}{P_m\widetilde h_m}
=\frac r2 FA\sum_qg_q^2+
\eta\ip{P_m(f\,\text{centered time})}{P_m\widetilde h_m}
\ge \frac r2 FA-C_0 .
\]
The last retained input has $g^2=1$; the slope term is uniformly
bounded since $m\le L_z$. No entrywise positivity of $P_m$ is assumed.
Thus the total late cross is at least
$rF\eta\varepsilon(1-\varepsilon)H^2/(4L_z)-O(H)$.
Early intervals contain only inputs before $J_0$; contraction and
Cauchy give total early cross at least
$-(r/2)F\eta\varepsilon^2H^2-O(H)$.
Also $\|P\widetilde h\|^2\le r^2F(H+n_0)/4=O(H)$.
Evaluation at this single legal full path yields
\[
\begin{split}
O_H-G_H(\pi)&\ge
\frac12\|(I-P)\theta\|^2+
\ip{P\theta}{P\widetilde h}-\frac12\|P\widetilde h\|^2\\
&\ge \frac{rF\eta}{2}
 \left[\frac{\varepsilon(1-\varepsilon)}{2L_z}
       -\varepsilon^2\right]H^2-O(H)
=\frac{rF\eta\varepsilon}{8L_z}H^2-O(H).
\end{split}
\]
Both cases are uniform in the calendar. Taking a smaller positive
constant than the minimum of the two displayed coefficients proves
the lower bound, and completes the theorem.
\end{proof}
